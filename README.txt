LECCORTUBE by Leccorside Ai
======================
Local AI video engine: script in, finished YouTube video out (voice, images, video scenes, music, thumbnail,
title, description and tags). Runs on your own PC, no subscription.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- NVIDIA RTX graphics card (RTX 20 series or newer). 8 GB VRAM works, 12 to 16 GB is great, 24 GB is best.
- 16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB recommended for video scenes
- About 60 GB free disk space (the AI models are big)
- Internet for the first setup

START
1. Double-click LECCORTUBE.exe (or the desktop shortcut).
   The first start prepares the engine (about 1 minute). Keep the black window open while you work.
2. LECCORTUBE opens Setup Center. It shows your GPU and picks the best settings for it.
   Click Install on the Core items: AI engine, FFmpeg, ComfyUI (or pick your existing ComfyUI folder),
   and the FLUX.2 Klein image model. Add Wan 2.2 for video scenes and Natural voices if you want them.
   Downloads continue where they stopped if the internet drops. Files you already have are never downloaded again.
3. Click New video, choose a type, paste your script, pick a voice and a style, then Build everything.

ALREADY USE COMFYUI?
Setup Center > Pick my ComfyUI folder. LECCORTUBE uses that ComfyUI and all the models already in it.

TIPS
- Voice Studio: listen to every voice with effects (Calm, Soft ASMR, Deep narrator, Cinematic...) before you build.
- Output folder: Settings > Choose, and every finished video is copied there.
- Delete a project from its page: the project files on your PC are removed too.
- Closing the black window stops LECCORTUBE and the ComfyUI it started.

UNINSTALL
Windows Settings > Apps > LECCORTUBE. Your projects, ComfyUI and models are kept; delete the folder yourself
if you want the space back.
